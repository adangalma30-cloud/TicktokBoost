# TickTokBoost — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-12_

TickTokBoost is a creator discovery and social exchange app. This policy explains
what data the app handles and how. **Short version: TickTokBoost stores
everything on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your data.**

## Data we store

All app data — your profile, coins, transactions, trust statistics, referrals,
uploaded photos/videos, settings and activity — is stored **locally on your
device** using Android's private app storage. It never leaves your device.

- **No accounts on our servers.** Sign-up creates a local profile only.
- **No analytics.** The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.
- **No tracking.** We do not track you across apps or websites.
- **No data selling.** We have no data to sell.

## What the app can access and why

| Access | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet | Only to open links you tap (e.g., a creator's TikTok profile in your browser/TikTok app). No TickTokBoost servers are contacted. |
| Storage (photos/media) | Only when YOU choose a profile picture or upload your own content. Files stay on your device. |
| Clipboard | Only when YOU tap "Copy" on your referral code/link. |

## Content you upload

Photos and videos you add to your creator profile are copied into the app's
private storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the
app deletes them.

## Deleting your data

Use **Settings → Account → Delete account** (erases everything immediately), or
uninstall the app. There is nothing to delete on any server because nothing was
ever sent to one.

## Children

TickTokBoost is intended for users 13+ (the same minimum age as the platforms
creators publish on).

## Contact

Questions about this policy: **support@ticktokboost.app**

## Changes

This app is in active development. When a future version introduces real
accounts or cloud features, this policy will be updated and the app will ask
for your consent before any data leaves your device.
